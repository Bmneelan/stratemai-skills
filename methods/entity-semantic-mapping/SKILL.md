# Entity and Semantic Mapping

Stub. Full build is Phase 2 and 3 of the Wayfinder plan.

## Purpose

Show how the client and its products exist as entities: what they are called, what is claimed about them, where those claims live, and where they conflict. Naming drift and contradictory source pages show up here before they show up in answers.

## Stages

To be defined in Phase 2. Inputs it will draw on: the evidence file's corpus, link graph, structured data and external knowledge sections (`research/schemas/evidence-file.schema.json`), and the transcript-fidelity instrument for spoken content.

Source of record for the current public description: stratemai.com.
