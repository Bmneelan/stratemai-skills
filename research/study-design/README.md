# Study designs

One file per study design. Each says what kind of study it governs, where its rules came from, and the checklist to clear before the first pull.

| Design | Governs | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `ai-answer-naming.md` | Whether and how AI answers name a company or product across a frozen prompt set | Sep 2026 calibration runs (HVAC, growth-marketing help) |

Add a design when a study measures something these rules don't cover (for example, claim accuracy over time, or spoken-content fidelity at scale). Shared rules for every design live in `../evidence-lanes/`.
